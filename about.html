About

T3rnel Browser is built by T3raTech Solutions (Pvt) Ltd. It is a browser extension with 98 tools and no servers behind it: what it does, it does on your machine.

What we make

ProductWhat it isWhere to get it
T3rnel Browser The extension. 98 tools for inspecting and automating web pages, free with a one-time Pro tier. t3ratech.github.io/t3rnel-browser-plugin
MCP Session Bridge The free MCP server that hands those tools to your AI client, driving the browser you are already signed into. npm

Install the extension

Pick your browser. Installing is free and takes one click; the Pro tier is a one-time purchase you can make later from inside the extension.

Support varies by browser — the overview says exactly where and why.

Add it to your AI client

The bridge is free and needs no licence:

npm install -g @t3ratech/mcp-session-bridge
mcp-session-bridge --install

Then point your MCP client at mcp-session-bridge. The MCP tools page lists everything it exposes, and the bridge's own session_install tool repeats these steps to your assistant at any time.

How we work

  • Nothing leaves your device. No account, no analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting. The privacy policy lists the only three cases where the extension makes a network request at all.
  • Pro is verified offline. Your licence is checked once at activation and on your own device after that.
  • No evasion, ever. There is no fingerprint spoofing, user-agent rotation or anti-bot circumvention in this product, and there will not be.
  • We say where it is weak. Partial browser support and the limits of every safety feature are written down rather than left to be discovered.
