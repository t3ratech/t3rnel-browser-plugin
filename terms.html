What the free account is

T3rnel Browser is free to install. The browser-control tools an AI uses to drive a page need no account and are not limited. The extension's own tools need an account: a free account may use the free ones three times a month, and the Pro tools need Pro. The allowance renews on the first of each month (UTC), is counted by our service so that reinstalling does not reset it, is not a trial and does not expire. Anonymous usage events are part of the product; the privacy policy lists what they carry and what they never carry.

What you buy

T3rnel Browser Pro is a one-time purchase of $29.99 USD, at the price shown at checkout. It is not a subscription, and it does not renew. Buying it grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Pro features of T3rnel Browser in up to five browsers that you sign in to with your account, on any machine you control. A licence key activated before 1.4.0 keeps working on the up to three installations it was activated on.

  • Your licence key is shown to you once, when payment completes. Keep it: it can be linked to your account from Settings if you bought under a different email.
  • Pro follows your account: any browser you sign in to, up to five, is Pro. The entitlement is verified on your device, and a signed-in Pro browser keeps working offline for fourteen days plus a grace period.
  • A browser can be signed out from the extension's settings, here or from another browser, which frees its seat for another.

Some features are described as safety measures — the approval gate, the LLM firewall, the encrypted vault, secret redaction. They are mitigations, not guarantees. They reduce a risk; they do not remove it. Do not rely on any of them as the only thing standing between an automated agent and something that matters.
