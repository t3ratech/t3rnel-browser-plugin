How licensing works

Payment goes through PayNow's hosted checkout, initiated and signed by our server — the extension never holds a payment credential, because an extension bundle is readable by anyone who installs it.

When you activate, your installation generates a cryptographic key that cannot be exported by any script, and receives a licence bound to it. Every start after that verifies offline, with no network call and no telemetry.

This means copying the extension's stored data to another machine does not move your licence — the key stays behind. It also means you can work entirely offline for weeks at a time.

We are straightforward about the limits of this in our licensing documentation, including what a determined user can still do. Software that runs on a machine you control can ultimately be modified by you; we would rather say so than imply a guarantee we cannot keep.
