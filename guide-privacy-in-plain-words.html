What T3rnel Browser stores and sends, in plain words

T3rnel Browser collects nothing by default: no account, no error reporting, no tracking. Everything it saves stays on your device, and only two opt-in features — an anonymous per-tool usage counter and a newsletter — can send anything, and both are off until you turn them on.

Updated 2026-10-09 · T3rnel Browser 1.3.0

The short version

There is no server that receives your browsing data. The extension makes network requests only when you start them, when a switch you control is on, as part of a purchase or licence you began, or to read something public. Every situation is listed in the full privacy policy.

What stays on your device

  • Settings and approval policies
  • Screenshot history and visual-regression baselines
  • Recorded sessions and generated test code
  • Vault entries, encrypted with a key derived from your master passphrase
  • Your licence entitlement, if you buy Pro

What you can opt into

An anonymous per-tool usage counter and a newsletter. Both are off until you say otherwise.

Questions

Where is my data stored?

Locally in your browser: settings, approval policies, screenshot history, baselines, recorded sessions, generated test code, encrypted vault entries and your licence. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

Does Pro phone home?

Activating a licence contacts the WavePay licensing service once, with your licence key and a public key for the installation. After activation your licence is verified offline.

Can my vault be read by the developer?

No. Vault entries are encrypted with a key derived from your master passphrase and the plaintext is never written.
